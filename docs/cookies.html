Cookies

This site sets no cookies. That is the whole policy.

No cookies, no banner

We set no cookies on this website, first-party or otherwise. There is no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising, no session tracking and no fingerprinting. That is why you were never asked to accept anything: there is nothing to accept.

Nothing stored in your browser

The site does not use local storage or session storage either. The live demo on the home page keeps the tasks you type in memory only, so refreshing the page starts it over.

The app launcher

The launcher in the top corner is loaded from apps.lightmorphic.com, one of our own addresses. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

Todom itself

The self-hosted app does not use cookies for tracking. Your tasks live in a file on your own server, not in your browser.

If any of that ever changes, this page changes first. Questions to privacy@lightmorphic.com.
