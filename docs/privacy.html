Privacy

This website collects nothing about you, and Todom itself keeps your tasks on your own server.

This website

todom.lightmorphic.com is a handful of static files. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no forms. Nothing you do here is recorded by us, because there is nothing here to record it with. The demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser and is thrown away the moment you leave.

One thing does come from another address: the app launcher in the top corner that lists Lightmorphic's other apps. It is served from apps.lightmorphic.com, which is ours. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and sends nothing back. Loading it does mean that server receives your IP address and browser details, the same as any request for a file.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details in its own server logs in order to deliver the page. We do not see those logs and cannot connect them to anyone. That processing is GitHub's, under its own privacy statement.

The app

Todom is self-hosted. You run it, on your own machine or server, and your tasks are written to a JSON file on your own disk. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours involved. We never receive your tasks, and there is no copy of them for us to lose, hand over or be asked for.

Todom makes no outbound connections of its own. It does not phone home, and it does not check for updates unless you go and look.

What happens to your tasks once they are on your server is in your hands. If other people can reach your Todom, they can see and change the list, so put it behind whatever protection suits you.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. If you think that is wrong, or you want to ask about any of this, write to privacy@lightmorphic.com and you will get a reply within a week.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
